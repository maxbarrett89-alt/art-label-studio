Barcode & QR images (ZIP)

One PNG per piece, named by SKU, e.g. WP-PAS-0001_barcode.png and WP-PAS-0001_qr.png. You can upload these to your website, add them to product photos, or use them in Canva or a label program.

Online store product file (CSV)

The SKU becomes both the product SKU and its barcode field, so scanning a label at your POS finds the right item. The description is written from medium, support, size, period and condition. A Photo link column starting with https:// is used as the product image.
